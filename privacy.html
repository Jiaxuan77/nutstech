Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated 20 September 2026

Who we are

Nuts Tech is responsible for the personal information described in this notice. We design and run curated executive delegations and cross-border programmes.

If you have any question about this notice or about how we handle your information, write to contact@nutstech.io.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the contact form, you give us:

  • your name and work email address;
  • your organisation, and the market or country you are asking about;
  • the type of enquiry, your preferred timing and approximate group size;
  • whatever you choose to tell us about what you want to achieve.

Only your name, email address and the description of your objective are required. The rest helps us answer usefully, and you can leave those fields empty.

If you email us or message us directly, we hold that correspondence too.

Like almost every website, ours also records technical information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. Our hosting provider keeps these server logs for security and reliability. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics profiling on this site.

Why we use it, and on what basis

PurposeBasis
Replying to your enquiry and preparing a proposalTaking steps at your request before entering a contract
Running a programme you have bookedPerforming our contract with you
Occasional follow-up about programmes related to your enquiryOur legitimate interest in developing our business, and you may object at any time
Keeping our website secure and workingOur legitimate interest in protecting the service
Meeting accounting, tax and legal obligationsCompliance with a legal obligation

We do not sell your information, rent it, or use it for advertising. We do not make decisions about you by automated means.

Who else sees it

We keep the circle small. Your information is handled by:

  • Our team, meaning the people working on your enquiry or programme.
  • Our form and email providers, which deliver your enquiry to our inbox and store our correspondence.
  • Our website host, which serves this site and keeps the server logs described above.
  • Programme partners, where delivering a booked programme requires it. This can include hotels, transport operators, interpreters and the organisations we are visiting, which may need a name and passport details for site access. We tell you before this happens and share only what is needed.
  • Professional advisers or authorities, where the law requires it.

Where it goes

We operate between China, Hong Kong and the markets we serve, and our providers are based in several countries. Your information will therefore be transferred outside the country you are writing from, including to countries whose data protection laws differ from your own. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we put appropriate ones in place.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for 24 months after our last contact with you, because business conversations about delegations often restart a year or more later. Records connected to a programme we delivered are kept for as long as accounting and tax rules require, normally seven years. Ask us to delete your information sooner and we will, unless we are legally required to keep it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information;
  • restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it;
  • transfer it to another provider;
  • stop sending you anything, at any time.

Write to contact@nutstech.io and we will reply within 30 days. Exercising these rights is free, and we will not treat you differently for it. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Keeping it safe

This site is served over an encrypted connection, access to enquiry data is limited to the people who need it, and we review our providers. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affected your information and posed a real risk to you, we would tell you and the relevant authority.

Children

This site is for business audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be flagged clearly on this page.
